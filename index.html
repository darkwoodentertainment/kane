Questions

How do I watch after buying?

After checkout you’re taken to the film’s viewing page, where you can press play straight away. To watch again later, use “Log in to watch” on this page with the email you purchased with.

Do I pay once or subscribe?

You pay once, with no subscription. After buying, you can stream the film at home whenever you like.

Which devices can I use?

Watch in an up-to-date web browser, such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge, on your computer, phone or tablet. Sign in with the email you bought the film with.

To watch on your TV, cast from Chrome with a Chromecast, use AirPlay from an iPhone, iPad or Mac, or connect your laptop to the TV with an HDMI cable. Smart TV web browsers and TVs with Chromecast built in aren’t supported.

The film plays in 5.1 surround where your browser, device and sound system support it. Otherwise it plays in stereo.

I’m having trouble with my purchase

Vimeo handles all payments, viewing access and purchase support for the film. Visit the Vimeo OTT help centre and their team will help you.

Can I screen the film at an event?

Your purchase is for personal viewing at home. To show COMMON WEALTH to an audience, such as in a cinema, hall, school, club, festival or event, you need a screening licence. This applies whether or not you charge for entry.

To arrange one, email ciao@darkwoodentertainment.com with your venue, date and expected audience size.
